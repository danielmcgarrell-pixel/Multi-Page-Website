Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

Make the most of your visit with this sample itinerary featuring some of Davao's popular attractions and experiences.

Day Time Activity Location
Day 1 Morning Explore the city and visit local landmarks. Davao City
Afternoon Discover wildlife conservation and local nature attractions. Philippine Eagle Center
Evening Enjoy local dishes and explore the city's food scene. Davao City
Day 2 Morning Enjoy the beach and coastal scenery. Dahican Beach, Mati
Afternoon Relax by the coast and take photographs of the scenery. Mati, Davao Oriental
Evening Enjoy a local dinner and rest after exploring. Mati City
Day 3 Morning Visit the spectacular Aliwagwag Falls. Cateel, Davao Oriental
Afternoon Explore nearby natural attractions and scenic viewpoints. Davao Oriental
Evening Shop for local products and prepare for departure. Based on your return route

Travel note: This is a sample itinerary, not a fixed tour package. Davao City, Mati, and Cateel are far apart, so plan your transportation carefully. Consider dedicating additional days to destinations in Davao Oriental. Check local conditions, opening hours, and transportation availability before your trip.

Travel Tips

  • Plan your transportation: Check bus schedules, fares, travel times, and available local transportation before visiting distant attractions.
  • Check the weather: Bring suitable clothing and prepare for rain, especially when visiting mountains, waterfalls, and coastal areas.
  • Bring enough cash: Some smaller establishments and rural destinations may not accept digital payments.
  • Respect local culture: Follow local customs and ask permission before photographing people or cultural activities.
  • Protect the environment: Dispose of waste properly and follow conservation guidelines when visiting natural attractions.
  • Book accommodations early: Reserve your lodging in advance, particularly during festivals and busy travel periods.
  • Keep emergency contacts accessible: Save important contact numbers and let someone know your itinerary.
